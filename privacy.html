Privacy

reese.biz and the included tools do not collect personal data. However, they rely on third-party services that operate according to their own policies:

  • Cloudflare (DNS and CDN)
  • GitHub Pages (hosting)

Torchfinder

Torchfinder may display cover thumbnails hotlinked directly from third-party storefronts, crowdfunding sites, and publisher websites, such as DriveThruRPG, itch.io, and Kickstarter. If your browser loads these images, servers they depend on can receive a request that can include your IP address. Thumbnails are not loaded without your explicit consent, which you can grant or withdraw at any time in the app.

Torchtimer

Torchtimer has a "Go Live" feature in which timer state is temporarily stored in Cloudflare Workers KV to enable real-time session sharing, expiring after 24 hours. No personal data can be included in this state: torch labels, for example, are not even transmitted over live sessions by design, only through query parameters in the URL.
